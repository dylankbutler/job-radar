01 — Input

Two files define everything

profile.txt is a plain-language description of who you are — your background, what you're drawn to, where you want to work, and what you're actively trying to avoid. companies.txt is your targeted watchlist, one company per line. Edit either file in the repo and the next run picks it up.

02 — Trigger

Fires every morning while you sleep

A scheduled workflow on GitHub's servers runs automatically at 8am Pacific — nothing on your machine, no tab to keep open. You can also trigger it manually any time from the repo's Actions tab.

03 — Watchlist Scan

Every company on your list, checked

For each company in your watchlist, Claude searches their careers page and major job boards with your profile as context. It reads actual job descriptions — not just keywords — and surfaces up to 3 current openings per company that genuinely fit.

05 — Scoring

Every role gets a fit score

Claude scores each result from 0 to 100 based on how closely the role aligns with your profile — skills, level, culture, and location. The radar rings on each card reflect the score: green is a strong match, gold is reasonable, red is weak. Results sort highest-first.

06 — Dashboard

Results merge, dismissed roles stay gone

Each run merges new results with previous ones — so a strong role won't disappear just because it wasn't filled yet. Save roles you're tracking (stored in your browser). Dismiss ones that aren't right and they won't reappear. Your radar sharpens over time as you curate it.
